## Industry Overview

- Tesla competes on vehicle software, charging network scale, and battery integration against BYD, legacy automakers, and energy firms. [1]
- Tesla combines large-scale battery electric vehicle (BEV) manufacturing with integrated energy storage and software monetization. [1]
- Tesla remained a leading global BEV player by revenue and among the top two by volume; in 2023 it delivered approximately 1.81M vehicles—about 19–20% of global BEV unit share. [1]
- BYD is Tesla’s biggest global competitor, holding 17.1% of global EV market share in March 2026 versus Tesla’s 8.9%. [3]
- In 2024, BYD manufactured 4.03 million EVs, significantly outpacing Tesla's 1.79 million units. [5]
- Tesla’s U.S. EV market share was 59% in Q4 2025. [3]
- Tesla’s industry position remains strong in North America and utility-scale storage, but rising competition in China and parts of Europe increases execution risk. [1]
- Tesla holds a $1.46 trillion market cap, larger than Toyota, BYD, GM, and Ford combined. [3]
- Tesla’s main competitors in 2026 include BYD, Toyota, General Motors, Ford, Volkswagen Group, Rivian, Lucid, NIO, XPeng, and Li Auto. [3]

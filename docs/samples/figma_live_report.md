# Figma Research Report

## Company Overview

- Figma, Inc. develops and sells a collaborative, browser-based platform for designing, prototyping, building digital experiences, and subscriptions for access to its platform. [8]
- Figma Design is a collaborative design tool for teams that explore ideas and gather feedback, build realistic prototypes, and streamline product development with design systems. [8]
- Dev Mode allows developers to inspect designs and translate them into code without changing the design file. [8]
- FigJam is an online whiteboarding tool used to define ideas, align decisions, and move work forward. [9]
- Figma Slides is a presentation tool built for designers and their teams. [8]
- Figma Draw is a tool to create expressive designs with illustration tools. [8]
- Figma Buzz publishes brand templates to create social media assets, display ads, one-pagers, and others. [8]
- Figma Sites enables users to design, prototype, and publish. [8]
- Payload CMS is an open-source, headless content management system and application framework acquired by Figma. [8]
- Figma Make is an AI tool to design and prompt way to a functional prototype. [8]
- Figma Weave is for AI-powered media generation and editing. [8]
- Figma was incorporated in 2012 and is headquartered in San Francisco, California. [8]
- Figma launched Figma Design in 2015 using WebGL technology to bring design into the browser for the first time. [9]
- Figma launched FigJam in 2021. [9]
- Figma launched Dev Mode in 2023. [9]
- Figma introduced Figma Slides in 2024. [9]
- Figma completed its IPO in July 2025. [11]
- Figma has 1,886 employees. [11]
- Figma's stock symbol is FIG. [11]

## Industry Overview

- Figma operates in the Collaborative Design And Prototyping industry. [2]
- Figma's primary competitors in UI/UX design and prototyping include Sketch, InVision, and Adobe XD. [3]
- Figma's top competitors in the Collaborative Design And Prototyping category are Adobe Discover (18.96% market share), Adobe Premiere Pro CC (16.33%), and Adobe XD (9.33%). [2]
- Other notable Figma competitors include Adobe, Canva, Framer, Lucidspark, Miro, Mural, Penpot, Sketch, UxPin, Axure, and InVision. [5]
- Figma holds a 41.78% estimated market share in its category and ranks #1. [2]
- Figma is described as a collaborative interface design tool. [2]

- Figma's revenue for the third quarter of 2025 was $274.2 million, representing a 38% year-over-year increase. [1]
